Brand illustration & AI workflow

On Brand Illustrations in Ten Minutes, by Anyone

Only product designers at Driversnote could make brand illustrations.

It took days per asset. Results drifted with whoever made them. Nothing was documented.

I led the project, holding workshops at each milestone, and built a system anyone could run.

An Illustration Spec the Brand Should Have Had All Along

The brand had a typography spec and a colour spec; illustrations had drifted because nothing constrained them.

I broke a customer sentiment, "Mature Simplicity Built on Trust & Accuracy," into four characteristics: aesthetic tone, form language, colour and light, character design. Aesthetic tone was clean, calm, competent; characters were adult. The four characteristics did for illustration what the type spec had always done for type: bound the choices.

Those four characteristics filtered every illustration that followed.

The Sweet Spot Between Brand Fit and Production Speed

The rules narrowed the field, and Midjourney still returned thousands of plausible answers.

I mapped tone to surfaces, with expressive for marketing, balanced for empty states, and functional for in-product. The team plotted references against a second axis of brand alignment versus production effort and dot-voted the survivors. I built the workflow around ten minutes: an expression level, three Midjourney runs, a Figma pass.

The search resolved to a single style reference code, written into the shipped quick guide.

Ten Minutes, No Designer in the Loop

The output is a documented system, not a one-off illustration.

I documented the workflow in Figma. The asset bank holds more than a hundred illustrations across the spectrum. Marketing produces what marketing needs, product fills its own empty states, and the design team writes the system instead of staffing the queue.

Customer phrases became style characteristics, then a framework, then ten minutes of work anyone in the company could do.

How this came together

The steps, decisions, and tradeoffs behind it.

Discover Define Develop Deliver

01·Discover

Mining customer language

Clean, accurate, not childish. Mature simplicity built on trust and accuracy.

02·Discover

Team curation workshop

Each participant presented three references and the room voted on the board.

03·Define

Writing illustration’s spec

I wrote four characteristics: tone, form, colour and light, character design.

04·Define

Mapping expression to UX

I matched each expression position to marketing, app, and product surfaces.

05·Develop

Style fit mapping

The team plotted every reference on brand fit and production fit, then voted.

06·Develop

Midjourney style hunt

I searched Midjourney’s style library and found ten boards with reference codes.

07·Develop

Cutting ten to one

Four of ten candidates survived two criteria; I chose the one marked mature.

08·Deliver

Timing the workflow

I generated in Midjourney and refined in Figma, ten minutes total.

09·Deliver

Handing over the system

Three deliverables shipped: a workflow, guidelines, and an illustration bank.
